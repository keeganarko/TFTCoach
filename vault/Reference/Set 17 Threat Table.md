# Set 17 Threat Table — what each meta board demands from YOUR position

Verified mechanics (blitz.gg / metabot.gg / games.gg / Mobalytics Set 17 guides). NOTE FOR THE ENGINE: the Meta feed's 'Assassin Trait' is the internal name TFT17_AssassinTrait = **Rogue**.

## Trait mechanics that drive positioning
- **Rogue** (Talon, Briar, Gwen, Fizz, Kai'Sa, Riven): jump to enemy backline at combat start; below 50% HP, redirect attackers to a nearby unit, preferring tanks. → PM-1 responses.
- **Sniper** (Ezreal, Gnar, Samira, Xayah, Jhin, + Vex/Kindred/MF range 6): damage amp + per-hex amp — (2) 18%+2%/hex, (3) 24%+3%/hex, (4) 28%+4%/hex, (5) 32%+5%/hex. → PM-2 compression.
- **Challenger** (Bel'Veth, Jinx, Diana, Kindred): on target death, dash to a new target with +50% AS for 2.5s. Not combat-start access, but chain-dashes reach your backline mid-fight. → don't feed early kills near your carry; no dying chaff beside it.
- **Dark Star**: black hole consumes enemies at 10% max HP (execute) — kill-order threat, not positioning; don't leave executable stragglers.

## Unit ability shapes (the AoE map for PM-3)
- **Aurelion Sol**: expanding black hole, board-wide magic damage → BOARD-WIDE: never spread for it; Shroud his column (15/75 mana) or burst him.
- **Morgana**: chains ALL enemies in a radius, stun + stat steal → radius: cluster ≤2, checkerboard backline.
- **Blitzcrank**: disco ball at your LARGEST CLUMP, 3-hex-radius damage + knockup; passive bolt on highest-HP nearby enemy → keep clusters ≤2 or bait with a 3-tank clump on the far flank.
- **Gnar** (boulder, line stun), **Viktor** (death ray line), **Jhin** (final shot pierces a line): column offsets, never stack a column.
- **Jinx**: rocket splash on basic attacks → don't hug your frontline together.
- **Xayah**: feathers recall through the CLOSEST 3 enemies, stunning multi-hit targets → don't line up between your front and her.
- **Samira**: fires at the TWO LOWEST-HP enemies, resets on takedowns → PM-6: bench chaff, no low-HP units fielded.
- **Akali**: dashes THROUGH her target → she ends up behind your front; keep a body between her landing side and your carry.

## Top meta boards (from vault Meta S/A tier) → your response
- **Stargazer Mountain / Riven** (S, 3.99): fields Talon + Riven (Rogues) AND Xayah corner sniper → carry-front or saturate (PM-1) AND don't max your depth (Xayah/Jhin distance amp). Their Jhin r4c2: avoid column-stacking on c2's mirror.
- **Mana Trait / MF / Viktor** (S, 4.1): Zoe + Aurelion Sol + Viktor → column-offset vs Viktor ray; do NOT spread for Asol; Shroud targets Asol's column.
- **DRX / Kindred** (S, 4.15): Akali dash + Kindred range-6 + melee Marauder mass → full wall / refuse-flank (PM-4); no dying chaff (Kindred Challenger resets).
- **Mana Trait / MF / Ornn** (S, 4.19): Rhaast/Aatrox/Maokai melee weight → TL-1 wall; MF range 6 punishes depth → r3 backline.
- **Space Groove / Samira** (S, 4.24): Samira lowest-HP hunts (PM-6) + Blitzcrank clump ball → clusters ≤2, bench chaff, bait-clump on far flank.
