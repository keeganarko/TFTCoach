# Scout every carousel and elimination

Use carousel rounds and elimination pauses to read every board: who contests your line,
who is strongest, what damage types are coming. Comp and positioning decisions made blind
are guesses.

## Why
Unit pools are shared — a contested line means fewer copies for both players. Positioning
against the strongest boards (carry placement vs. assassins/backline access) is only
possible if you've seen them.

## When it applies / doesn't
Always applies; the cost is zero. Depth varies: full scout at 2-1/3-2/4-1 decision points,
quick threat-check otherwise.
