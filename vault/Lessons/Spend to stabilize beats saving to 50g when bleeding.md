# Spend to stabilize beats saving to 50g when bleeding

Interest breakpoints are a default, not a rule. When losing 10+ HP per round with a weak
board, roll/level to stabilize — the HP (and streak gold swing) is worth more than 2-3
interest per round.

## Why
HP is a resource you can't buy back; it buys later greed (losing HP now removes your
ability to econ later). Interest lost by dropping from 50g to 30g is ~2g/round; a lost
round at stage 3+ costs 8-15 HP and streak gold.

## When it applies / doesn't
- Applies: stage 3+, HP below ~60, losing consistently, board more than one tier behind lobby.
- Doesn't: intentional loss-streak openers (stage 1-2), or when rolling can't realistically
  improve the board (units contested/none in pool).

## Evidence
- [[2026-05-25 Game]] — saved at 27g/level 5, bled out; rolling was correct.
- [[2026-05-24 Game]] — inverse case: saving to 50g was right because the board was holding.
