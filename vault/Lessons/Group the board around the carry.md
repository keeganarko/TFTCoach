# Group the board around the carry

Position as a unit: frontline absorbs, backline funnels protection to the itemized carry.
A split board fights two half-battles and loses both.

## Why
Fights are decided by whether the carry survives long enough to cast/ramp. Spreading units
dilutes tank aggro-holding and lets divers reach the carry uncontested.

## When it applies / doesn't
- Applies: default for nearly all comps.
- Doesn't: deliberate splits vs. AoE wombo setups, corner-dodging assassins/artillery,
  or units whose traits want spread. These are exceptions made *after* scouting, not habits.
