# TFT Brain — Index

This is the entry point the coach loads at the start of every game. It stays small:
principles inline, everything else linked and loaded on demand.

## How this vault works

- **[[Games]]** — one note per game, written automatically after each match (screenshot
  timeline + Riot match result). Raw material, never edited by hand.
- **Lessons** — atomic, testable claims distilled from games. Each has a `status`:
  `candidate` (seen once) → `confirmed` (supported by 3+ games or an authoritative guide)
  → promoted into a Principle, or `retired` (patch-bound and expired).
- **Principles** — the short list of evergreen rules that go into every coaching prompt.
  Hard cap: ~10. If a new principle earns its slot, an old one merges or retires.
- **Comps** — per-comp playbooks for the current set, patch-stamped. Auto-refreshed from
  meta sources; hand-annotated with personal experience.
- **Meta** — machine-written patch snapshots (tier lists, augment/item stats). Never edit;
  regenerated on every patch.

## Active principles

1. [[Spend to stabilize beats saving to 50g when bleeding]] — interest breakpoints are a
   default, not a rule. Losing 10+ HP per round costs more than lost interest.
2. [[Scout every carousel and elimination]] — comp decisions made blind are guesses;
   contested lines and threat boards change pivot/positioning calls.
3. [[Group the board around the carry]] — a split board loses fights it should win.
   Position tanks to protect the item-holder, not to look symmetric.
